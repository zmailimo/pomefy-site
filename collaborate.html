Who We Look For

We are not hiring for headcount. We are looking for people who bring a specific kind of value to client work: the ability to think commercially, work independently, and stay in the room until the outcome is real.

If you have done the work before, at a senior level, and want to do it with more ownership and less overhead, POME is built for you.

Who Thrives Here

Senior Consultants

You have led engagements end to end in branding, marketing, e-commerce, BD, or investment advisory. You can hold a client relationship and a deliverable at the same time.

Associates

You are early in your career but serious about the craft. You learn fast, write clearly, and want to work directly with senior people instead of being parked on a bench.

Specialists & Partners

You have deep expertise in one domain and want to bring it to client work without building a whole practice around it. We work with specialists on a project basis.

How We Engage

Direct client work from day one. No bench, no shadowing, no waiting your turn.

Senior autonomy. You own your engagements and your relationships.

A limited portfolio. We take on few clients at a time, so the work stays high quality.

Flexible arrangements. Full-time, part-time, fractional, and per-engagement.

A network, not just a job. POME is a place to build a practice around your strengths.

Our Process

1. Apply

Tell us who you are, what you do, and the kind of client work you want. The form below takes a few minutes.

2. Conversation

A first call with a POME consultant to understand your experience, your interests, and how you would fit into client work.

3. Engagement

If there is a fit, we bring you onto a project or a client relationship. We start small and grow from there.
