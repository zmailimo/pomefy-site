What We Do

Distribution Rights Acquisition

We identify, negotiate, and secure distribution rights for your brand in new markets and channels.

Distributor Identification & Vetting

We find distributors with real reach, real relationships, and the operational capacity to move your product, then we qualify them before you ever get on a call.

Deal Structuring & Negotiation

Territory, exclusivity, pricing, minimum order commitments, and renewal terms. We negotiate the agreement so it protects your brand and your margin.

Onboarding & Performance

We help get the distributor launched, then track sell-through so the relationship keeps producing instead of stalling after the first order.

How We Work

01

Understand the Brand and the Objective

What you sell, who buys it, where the margin sits, and which markets make sense next. We start with the commercial picture, not a target list.

02

Map the Right Markets and Distributors

We build a shortlist based on category fit, channel coverage, operational strength, and appetite for your product. Quality over volume, every time.

03

Negotiate and Secure the Rights

We handle outreach, positioning, and negotiation. Territory, exclusivity, pricing, and commitments get defined so you are not exposed.

04

Launch and Manage Performance

We support the distributor through onboarding, then track sell-through so the partnership produces beyond the first order.
