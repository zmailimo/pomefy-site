Too many businesses end up with a digital asset that never pays for itself.

It happens all the time. A business invests in a website, a platform, a full e-commerce build. The asset goes live. The invoices get paid. And then it sits there, costing money to maintain, returning nothing.

The problem was never the technology. The problem is that no one built the commercial engine around it. A digital asset without a selling system is not an asset. It is an expense.

POME exists to close that gap.

Why Digital Assets Fail

No commercial strategy behind the build

The site was designed as a project, not as a revenue channel. No pricing logic, no positioning, no path to purchase.

Tools installed, not configured

Software was chosen for features instead of fit. It works in theory and stalls in practice.

No one owns the number

The agency delivered, the developer shipped, and nobody stayed to own revenue.

Marketing disconnected from the storefront

Traffic arrives to a page that was never built to convert it.
