The AI Market Is Loud. Most of It Isn't for You.

Every week there is a new tool, a new model, a new promise that this one changes everything. Most businesses respond in one of two ways. They buy too much and end up with tools nobody uses. Or they buy nothing and fall behind on work that could be running better.

Neither is a strategy. What most companies need is a stack, not a subscription list. A small set of tools chosen for real reasons, connected to real workflows, and adopted by real people.

That is what POME builds.

What We Do

Stack Assessment

We map how your business actually works today, then identify where AI genuinely helps and where it does not. Most workflows should stay as they are.

Stack Design

A right-sized set of tools across writing, research, operations, customer service, and data, chosen for fit, cost, and how well they work together.

Integration & Configuration

Tools connected to your existing systems and tuned to your context, so they produce useful output instead of generic answers.

Adoption & Training

Team onboarding, prompt libraries, and internal guidelines, so the stack gets used instead of forgotten.

Our Process

1. Audit Your Workflows

We sit with your team and map the work: what gets done, by whom, how long it takes, and where the friction is. We are looking for tasks where AI changes the outcome, not just the tooling.

What you get: A workflow map with AI opportunities ranked by impact and effort.

2. Cut the Noise

We review the market against your actual needs and rule out the vast majority of it. A short list you can trust, with reasons for every inclusion and exclusion.

What you get: A shortlist of tools, each with a clear rationale and a cost estimate.

3. Build the Stack

We select the final tools, configure them to your context, and connect them to your existing systems. No loose ends, no half-finished integrations.

What you get: A working stack, documented, with access set up for the right people.

4. Train and Refine

We onboard your team, run training sessions, and refine the setup based on how it performs in real use. The stack should get better with time, not decay.

What you get: Trained team, prompt library, internal guidelines, and a review cadence.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell AI tools or take vendor commissions
We do not recommend AI where it does not belong
We do not build stacks that require a dedicated engineer to maintain
We recommend the smallest stack that solves the problem
We tell you when the answer is not AI at all
