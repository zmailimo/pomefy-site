Investment Advisory

Capital doesn't reward great businesses. It rewards ready ones.

Most founders who get turned down by investors don't have a bad business. They have an unready one. The numbers aren't clean, the story isn't sharp, and the governance isn't in place to survive due diligence.

What We Do

Valuations

Defensible valuation work grounded in your financials, your market, and comparable transactions, not a number pulled from a template.

Pitch Decks

Governance

Growth Readiness

Our Process

1. Assess Where You Stand

We review your financials, your cap table, your story, and your operations. We tell you honestly what is ready and what is not. No optimism, no padding, just an accurate picture of what an investor will see.

What you get: A written readiness assessment with the gaps ranked by how much they will cost you in a negotiation.

2. Fix What Investors Will Question

Clean numbers, tighter positioning, and governance gaps closed before you enter a room. This is often the longest stage, and the one that most determines the outcome.

What you get: Clean financials, a clear narrative, and the structural pieces an investor will ask for in diligence.

3. Build the Materials

Valuation, deck, and financial model, built to withstand scrutiny and to tell one consistent story. Nothing in the deck contradicts the model. Nothing in the model contradicts the valuation.

What you get: A valuation report, an investor deck, and a financial model that hold together under pressure.

4. Support the Raise

We stay in the room through investor conversations, diligence, and negotiation, so you are not navigating it alone. When questions come up, we help you answer them.

What you get: Senior support through the process, from first meeting to signed term sheet.
